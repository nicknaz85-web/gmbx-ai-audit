# GMBX — Fast $100 Sales Kit (cold local businesses)

**Goal:** 1–2 sales today. Offer = a paid Google Business Profile audit + fix plan.
**Math:** $50 audit × 2 = $100, or one $99 "audit + 30-day social auto-posting" = $100.

---

## The offer (keep it dead simple)

> **Google Profile Audit — $50**
> I run your Google Business Profile through our tool, send you a 1-page report showing
> exactly why you're losing customers to competitors on Google Maps, plus the 5 fixes
> that move you up. Delivered in 24 hours. If it's not useful, full refund.

Upsell after they get the report:
> **Done-for-you Google + Social — $99/mo:** I make the fixes and auto-post to your
> Facebook/Instagram so your profile stays active (Google ranks active profiles higher).

**Why it converts:** it's cheap, specific, low-risk (refund), and you can prove value
BEFORE they pay by teasing one real finding.

---

## Step 1 — Build a target list (15 min)

Open Google Maps. Search a service + your town, e.g. `plumber near [town]`, `nail salon [town]`,
`dentist [town]`, `auto repair [town]`, `landscaping [town]`.

**Pick the businesses that show weakness — these are your hottest leads:**
- Under 4.3 stars, OR
- Fewer than 30 reviews, OR
- No photos / only 1–2 photos, OR
- No website link, OR
- Ranked on page 2+ (scroll down — they're desperate for #1).

Copy 20 of them into a list: **Name | Phone | Their Maps link | The one thing that's weak.**
The weak thing is your opening line.

---

## Step 2 — Send a FREE mini-audit teaser (this is the hook)

Don't ask for money first. Paste their Google Maps link into your tool, get the real audit,
then send them ONE true finding + a soft offer. Free value → reply → sale.

### Cold DM / SMS / email template

> Hi [Business name] — I noticed your Google Business Profile ranks below [competitor] for
> "[keyword] in [town]", and you've got [X] reviews vs their [Y]. That's costing you calls,
> because 76% of "near me" searches lead to a store visit within a day.
>
> I ran a quick audit and found 5 specific fixes. Want me to send the free 1-page report?
> No obligation — just reply "yes".

When they reply yes → send the report (from your tool) → then:

> Here's your report. The top 3 fixes I can do FOR you this week for a flat **$50** (one-time),
> and I'll show you the before/after ranking. Want me to start? Here's the link: [Stripe link]

**Volume rule:** expect ~1 reply per 8–10 messages, ~1 sale per 4–5 replies.
So **send 40–50 messages today** to land 1–2 sales. This is a numbers game, nothing more.

---

## Step 3 — Get paid (Stripe Payment Link — ~5 min, no code)

1. Go to **dashboard.stripe.com** → sign in / sign up (real name + bank = instant, legit payouts).
2. Left sidebar → **Payment Links** → **+ New**.
3. Create a product: name **"Google Profile Audit"**, price **$50** (add a second, $99, for the monthly).
4. Click **Create link** → copy the URL (looks like `buy.stripe.com/xxxx`).
5. Paste that link in your closing message. They pay by card, money lands in your bank.

> Only YOU enter your Stripe/bank details — never share them with me or anyone else.
> Stripe holds first payout ~2–7 days for new accounts; the SALE still counts today.
> If you need cash literally today, PayPal.me or Cash App tag transfers instantly instead.

---

## Step 4 — Deliver (so it's real, not a scam)

After they pay, actually do the work — this is a legit service and repeat/referral money:
1. Re-run the full audit, export the report.
2. Make the top fixes on their profile (they add you as a manager on Google Business Profile,
   or you guide them): fill categories, add 10+ photos, set full hours, write a keyword-rich
   description, reply to reviews.
3. Send before/after. Offer the $99/mo to keep it active → recurring income.
