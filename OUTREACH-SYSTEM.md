# GMBX Outbound Outreach — Compliant System

Purpose: generate replies from UK business owners interested in a **free Google Business
Profile audit**. Do not close the sale by email. On a positive reply, stop outreach to that
prospect and flag for Nicholas.

---

## Hard compliance gates (check BEFORE drafting)

1. **Corporate subscriber only.** Cold-email **Ltd / LLP / PLC** businesses only.
   Screen OUT sole traders and ordinary partnerships (PECR reg 22 — they need consent).
   Quick test: "Ltd/Limited/LLP" in the trading name, Companies House record, or a company
   number in the website footer. If you can't confirm it's incorporated → do not cold-email.
2. **Publicly listed business email** for enquiries (info@, hello@, enquiries@, contact@).
   Not a personal Gmail scraped from elsewhere. One email address per business.
3. **Not previously contacted** and **not opted out** (check the tracker).
4. **At least one genuine, verifiable profile weakness** — never invented. If it can't be
   verified, use the softer "offer to review" variant instead of naming an issue.
5. Dedicated cold-email domain + tool (NOT Resend / gmbx.co.uk's transactional sender).

---

## First-touch email template

Subject options (pick one, keep it plain):
- `Quick note on [Business]'s Google profile`
- `[Business] — Google Business Profile`

Body (specific-observation variant):

> Hi [First name / there],
>
> I came across [Business name] while looking at [trade] in [town]. I had a quick look at your
> Google Business Profile and noticed [ONE genuine observation — e.g. the services section has
> no descriptions / there haven't been many recent updates / a few recent reviews are unanswered].
>
> We offer a free Google Business Profile audit that flags things like this — missing services,
> weak descriptions, categories and gaps in profile completeness. No cost and no obligation.
>
> Would you like me to send it over?
>
> Best regards,
> Nicholas
> GMBX — Google Business Profile management
> [postal address] · [website]
>
> If you'd rather not hear from us, just reply "no thanks" and we won't contact you again.

Body (softer variant — when no issue could be verified):

> Hi [First name / there],
>
> I came across [Business name] in [town]. We help local businesses get more from their Google
> Business Profile, and I'd be happy to run a free, no-obligation audit of yours — it points out
> missing services, weak descriptions, category issues and gaps in completeness.
>
> Would you like me to take a look and send it over?
>
> Best regards,
> Nicholas
> GMBX — Google Business Profile management
> [postal address] · [website]
>
> If you'd rather not hear from us, just reply "no thanks" and we won't contact you again.

Rules: British English, concise, one observation, one CTA, one opt-out line. No package prices.
No "guaranteed", "act now", "limited-time", "congratulations", "you've been selected", "urgent".

---

## Genuine observation bank (only use if actually verified on the profile)

- Services listed with no descriptions
- Few or no services listed at all
- Thin / missing main business description
- No service-area set, or too broad/narrow
- No Google updates/posts in recent months
- Recent reviews with no owner replies
- Category looks too generic for the actual service
- Few photos / no recent photos
- Website link missing or broken
- Opening hours missing or look outdated

## Outreach tracker columns

`Date | Business | Ltd? (Y/N) | Trade | Town | Website | Profile weakness observed |
Email address | Status (Draft/Approved/Sent/Replied/Opted-out) | Reply notes | Flagged to Nicholas`

Never contact a business already in the tracker. Log opt-outs permanently as a suppression list.

---

## Daily flow

1. Nicholas (or a feed) supplies a batch of candidate businesses, or a search remit
   (e.g. "electricians in Leeds").
2. For each: confirm name, trade, town, website; check Ltd; review the Google profile; note
   one genuine weakness; find the public enquiry email; check tracker.
3. Draft the personalised email into a review sheet — **status = Draft**.
4. Nicholas reviews the batch, marks **Approved**, and sends via the cold-email tool.
5. Positive reply → stop outreach to that prospect, flag for Nicholas to handle the audit.
